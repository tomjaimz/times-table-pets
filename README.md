# 🦉 Jemima's Magical Pet Academy — Times Tables (1–12)

A Duolingo-inspired, iPad-friendly web game built for **Jemima** to master her 1–12 times tables!

## ✨ Features
- **⚡ 3-Second Blitz Timer** (plus 5s Fast & 8s Warmup modes)
- **🔄 Sudden-Death Streak Runs**: Answer 10 in a row to unlock the Treasure Chest! Miss one or run out of time and the streak resets to `0` after a quick teaching pause showing the right answer.
- **🗺️ 4 Adventure Worlds**: 16 stepping-stone levels from `×1 & ×2` up to the `Grand 12×12 Master Crown`.
- **🐾 24 Collectible Magical Pets**: Hatch companions that cheer Jemima on during runs.
- **🎁 Magical Gem Shop & Golden Coupons**: Trade earned Gems for Mystery Pet Eggs or real-life rewards like **15 Minutes Extra Screen Time**.
- **📊 12×12 Mastery Heatmap & Metrics**: Tracks accuracy %, response speed (ms), tricky facts, and recent streak runs.

## 📱 Installing on Jemima's iPad
1. Open the GitHub Pages link in **Safari** on the iPad.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Launch **Jemima's Pets** from the Home Screen for a full-screen, native app experience!
